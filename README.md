CIPHER

Every clue hides a story.

CIPHER is an interactive digital investigation and mystery game where players take on the role of an investigator and solve challenging cases by analyzing digital evidence, discovering hidden clues, decoding information, and connecting seemingly unrelated events.

Players progress through a series of fictional cases involving suspicious messages, digital records, hidden files, mysterious identities, and unexpected twists. Each decision and discovery helps uncover the truth behind the case.

The game combines logic, observation, problem-solving, and digital investigation to create an engaging experience rather than a traditional quiz-based game.

Key Features

- 🕵️ Interactive investigation cases
- 🔍 Digital evidence and hidden clues
- 🔐 Cipher and puzzle-solving challenges
- 🧩 Logical reasoning tasks
- 📁 Evidence and case-file system
- ⏱️ Time-based challenges
- 🏆 Points, levels, and achievements
- 📊 Player progress and performance tracking
- 👤 Player accounts and profiles
- 🛠️ Admin panel for creating and managing cases

Technology

HTML • CSS • JavaScript • PHP • MySQL

The frontend provides the interactive game interface, JavaScript handles puzzles and real-time interactions, PHP manages the game logic and backend, and MySQL stores players, cases, evidence, scores, and progress.

# *HOW THE DECISION SYSTEM WORKS:*

1. Players investigate a case by exploring clues and evidence.
2. They can check messages, files, images, and suspect information.
3. At important moments, players choose what to investigate next.
4. Different choices can reveal new clues or lead to false trails.
5. Players collect evidence and connect the clues.
6. Their decisions affect their progress and score.
7. At the end, players use the evidence to solve the case.
8. The system rewards careful observation, logical thinking, and good decisions.

Simple flow:
Explore → Find Clues → Make Decisions → Collect Evidence → Solve the Case
